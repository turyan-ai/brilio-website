Questions

Frequently asked

How is a deep clean different from a standard clean?

A standard clean keeps a home in good shape. A deep clean adds the detail work, like baseboards, grout, vents, and built-up grime, so it takes longer.

How often do I need a deep clean?

Many homes get one every three to six months, with standard cleans in between. We also recommend one for your first visit with us.

Do I need to be home?

No. Many clients leave a key or door code. Tell us what works for you when you book.

What if I’m not happy with something?

Tell us within 24 hours and we’ll come back to re-clean it at no charge.
