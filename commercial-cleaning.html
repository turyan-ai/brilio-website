A plan built around your space

We agree on the tasks and how often each one happens at your walkthrough. Here’s what a typical plan covers.

Workspaces

  • Desks and work surfaces wiped
  • Trash and recycling emptied
  • Vacuuming and mopping

Restrooms

  • Toilets, urinals, and sinks sanitized
