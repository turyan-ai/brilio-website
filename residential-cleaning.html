Questions

Frequently asked

How often should I schedule a cleaning?

Most homes do well with biweekly cleaning. Busy households or homes with pets often choose weekly. We also do one-time and deep cleans whenever you need them.

What’s the difference between a standard and a deep clean?

A standard clean keeps a home in good shape. A deep clean adds detailed work like baseboards, inside appliances, and built-up grime. We recommend a deep clean for your first visit or before a move.

What if I’m not happy with something?

Let us know within 24 hours and we’ll come back to re-clean it at no charge.

Are you insured?

Yes. Brilio is insured and bonded.
