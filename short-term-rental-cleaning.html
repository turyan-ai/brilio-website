Questions

Frequently asked

Can you do same-day turnovers?

Yes. We work from 6 AM to midnight every day, so we can fit a turnover between an 11 AM checkout and a 4 PM check-in, including weekends and holidays.

How do you know when to clean?

Share your booking calendar with us and we schedule a turnover after every checkout. We also take one-off and last-minute requests by phone or text.

Do you restock supplies?

Yes. We restock toiletries, paper goods, and kitchen basics from the supplies you keep at the property, and let you know before anything runs out.

Will I know when the unit is ready?

Yes. After each turnover we send you photos of the finished unit, plus notes on any damage, maintenance issues, or missing items.

What happens if a guest leaves something behind?

We log it, send you a photo, and keep it safe at the property or with us until you tell us what to do with it.

Do you work with property managers who have several units?

Yes. Tell us how many units you manage and how often they turn over, and we’ll set up a schedule and a checklist for each property.
